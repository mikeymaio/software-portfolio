# Michael Maio — Portfolio

The personal portfolio site at michaelmaio.com: a single page that presents Michael as an engineer to the people deciding whether to hire him.

## Language

**Visitor**:
Someone reading the site. The primary Visitor is a hiring manager or engineering lead evaluating Michael for a senior full-time role; freelance clients are secondary.
_Avoid_: User, audience, recruiter (as a synonym)

**Positioning Line**:
The single sentence that states who Michael is professionally: a full-stack engineer who ships mobile apps and AI products end to end.
_Avoid_: Tagline, headline, title, bio

**Project**:
A piece of shipped work Michael contributed to, credited to the company or client it was built for.
_Avoid_: Work item, case study, portfolio piece

**Featured Project**:
One of the four or five most recent Projects whose product is still live, shown first and in full.
_Avoid_: Highlight, top project, hero project

**Retired Project**:
A Project whose product has been shut down or removed from its stores. A Retired Project is never Featured and links to nothing.
_Avoid_: Dead project, archived, old project

**Liner Notes**:
The credits-style presentation of a Project: its name, client, Michael's role, years, and the stack, written the way an album credits its players.
_Avoid_: Card, project details, metadata

**Signature**:
The one recurring design idea that makes the site memorable, drawn from Michael's background as a musician and audio engineer and applied with restraint: a waveform drawn like a clip in a recording session, partly played, plus the record-sleeve vocabulary of Liner Notes and "Side A / Side B".
_Avoid_: Theme, gimmick, branding
